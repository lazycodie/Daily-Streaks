Daily Streaks privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Applies to the Daily Streaks app for Android

In short: Daily Streaks does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet access. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The habits you create (name, icon, colour, time of day and weekly goal) and the days you check them off. This information is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or crash reporting.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other files.
  • It does not use Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer for your habit data.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
VibrateA short buzz when you check off a habit. You can turn it off in Style → Feel.

Exports and backups you choose to make

Export to Excel creates a spreadsheet on your device and opens Android's own "Save to" screen, so you choose where the file goes. Copy backup copies your habit data as text to the clipboard so you can keep it somewhere safe. Once you save or paste these, the destination you chose (for example a cloud drive or notes app) handles them under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete individual habits in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Daily Streaks → Storage.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions, use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
